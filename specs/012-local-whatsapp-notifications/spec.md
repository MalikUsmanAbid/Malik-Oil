# Free local WhatsApp notification collector
Owner authorizes work toward free continuous group-rate access, no billing/deployment. Implement opt-in Android 9+ notification reader for WhatsApp Business only, exact group-title allowlist and explicit group marker. No QR scraping, sending, cloud uploads, history retrieval or auto-approved rates. Device credential unlock is required for configuration/inbox. Store bounded raw notification messages encrypted locally under current actor, dedupe stable messages, ignore other apps/private messages/summaries. User must manually grant Android notification access and enter unique group name. Stop/reconnect/clear and checker actions. Do not claim 24/7 guarantee, API membership or physical-device success.
